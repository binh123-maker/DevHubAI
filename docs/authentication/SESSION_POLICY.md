# DevHub AI - Official Session & Device Policy

## Policy Standards & Requirements

### 1. Device Session Lifetime
- **Idle Expiration**: 30 days.
- **Device Metadata**: Captures IP Address, User-Agent, Operating System, Browser name, and Last Active Timestamp.

### 2. Concurrent Sessions & Revocation
- **Concurrent Logins**: Allowed across multiple devices.
- **Remote Revocation**: Users can list active sessions and revoke individual sessions remotely.
- **Global Invalidation**: Password reset or admin ban immediately invalidates all active sessions.
